General Terms of Service (GTS)

Professional contract governing the use of MyShifters matchmaking services

IMPORTANT NOTICE: These General Terms of Service (GTS) constitute a legally binding contract between the Client Establishment and MYSHIFTERS. By accepting these GTS, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and accepted all of their provisions. It is recommended to print and/or keep them.

ARTICLE 1 - PURPOSE AND DEFINITIONS

1.1 Purpose

These General Terms of Service (hereinafter the "GTS") aim to define the conditions under which the company MYSHIFTERS (hereinafter "the Platform") provides Client Establishments (hereinafter "the Client") with a matchmaking service with self-employed workers registered as sole proprietors (hereinafter "the Provider(s)") for the execution of temporary assignments (hereinafter "the Assignment") within the Client's establishment.

ROLE OF MYSHIFTERS: MYSHIFTERS acts exclusively as a technical and administrative matchmaking platform. MYSHIFTERS is not an employer, exercises no supervisory authority over Providers, and is not a party to the Assignment execution contract that is established directly between the Client and the Provider. MYSHIFTERS acts as a payment agent and matchmaking organizer.

1.2 Definitions

"Platform": Refers to the MYSHIFTERS digital service, accessible by any electronic means, enabling matchmaking between Clients and Providers.

"Client": Any hotel establishment, tourist residence, or leisure establishment that has subscribed to these GTS to order Assignments.

"Provider": Self-employed worker registered as a sole proprietor, having signed an Independent Service Agreement with MYSHIFTERS, and registered on the Platform to execute Assignments.

"Assignment": Temporary and specific service provision, defined by a position (e.g., receptionist, housekeeper), a date, specific hours, and an exact execution location, entrusted by a Client to a Provider via the Platform.

"Commission": Platform remuneration, corresponding to a percentage of the total Assignment amount, deducted from the payment made by the Client before transfer to the Provider.

"Assignment Contract": Direct and specific commercial agreement that is formed between the Client and the Provider upon the Provider's acceptance of an Assignment proposed via the Platform.

ARTICLE 2 - ACCEPTANCE AND REGISTRATION

2.1 Access to the Service is reserved for professionals. Any registration on the Platform constitutes full and complete acceptance of these GTS.

2.2 The Client guarantees the accuracy and truthfulness of the information provided during registration (company name, SIRET, address, legal representative).

2.3 The Client's account is personal and confidential. The Client is responsible for any use made of their account.

ARTICLE 3 - ASSIGNMENT ORDERING PROCESS

3.1 The Client submits an Assignment request via the Platform, by email, or by phone, specifying: the required position, date, hours, location, and any specific necessary skills.

3.2 MYSHIFTERS searches among its available and qualified Providers and proposes one or several profiles to the Client.

3.3 The Client selects the proposed Provider. This selection constitutes an Assignment offer.

3.4 MYSHIFTERS transmits the Assignment offer to the Provider. The Assignment is confirmed only when the Provider explicitly accepts it.

3.5 MYSHIFTERS notifies the Client of the Assignment confirmation, with the Provider's contact details.

ARTICLE 4 - CLIENT OBLIGATIONS

4.1 The Client undertakes to provide the Provider with a safe working environment compliant with applicable regulations.

4.2 The Client must welcome the Provider, provide them with the instructions and information necessary for the proper execution of the Assignment, as well as establishment-specific equipment if necessary (e.g., reservation software, badge).

4.3 The Client will treat the Provider with respect and acknowledge their status as an independent contractor. No supervisory relationship should be exercised.

4.4 The Client must report to MYSHIFTERS, as soon as possible, any problem occurring during the execution of the Assignment.

4.5 The Client undertakes not to enter into direct relations with a Provider met via the Platform to commission Assignments outside the Platform, for a period of 12 months following the last Assignment performed via MYSHIFTERS (non-circumvention clause).

ARTICLE 5 - PRICING, INVOICING AND PAYMENT

5.1 Pricing

Assignment prices are communicated to the Client before validation. They vary according to position, duration, timing (weekend, public holiday) and request lead time (emergency). Prices are indicated in Euros, all taxes included (VAT included).

5.2 Invoicing and Payment by the Client

5.2.1 MYSHIFTERS sends the Client an invoice for the total Assignment amount, upon confirmation of the Assignment.

5.2.2 The invoice payment is due upon receipt. The payment term is 30 days end of month from the invoice date.

5.2.3 Payment is made by bank transfer to the account indicated on the invoice.

5.2.4 Any late payment automatically entails penalties equal to three times the legal interest rate, and a fixed compensation of €40 for recovery costs.

5.3 Provider Remuneration

5.3.1 MYSHIFTERS transfers to the Provider the agreed amount for the Assignment, after deducting its Commission.

5.3.2 Absolute suspensive condition: The transfer only occurs after MYSHIFTERS receives a regular invoice issued by the Provider in the name of MYSHIFTERS, corresponding precisely to the executed Assignment.

5.3.3 The transfer period to the Provider is 7 working days after receipt of full payment from the Client by MYSHIFTERS.

FINANCIAL SCHEMA:

  1. Client pays €100 VAT included to MYSHIFTERS for an Assignment.
  2. MYSHIFTERS deducts its Commission (e.g., 20%).
  3. MYSHIFTERS receives the €80 invoice from the Provider.
  4. MYSHIFTERS transfers €80 to the Provider.

6.1.3 MYSHIFTERS is not responsible for cancellations, delays, or absences of a Provider, but undertakes to do its utmost to find a replacement as soon as possible.

6.2 Client Liability

The Client is solely responsible for verifying the Provider's skills for the Assignment, their welcome and supervision on site. The Client is responsible for the security of their property, premises, and own staff.

6.3 Insurance

6.3.1 MYSHIFTERS is covered by Professional Civil Liability insurance for its matchmaking activity.

6.3.2 Each Provider registered on the Platform must hold valid Professional Civil Liability insurance covering their activity. MYSHIFTERS verifies this insurance upon registration but cannot guarantee its maintenance. The Client may request a certificate from the Provider.

ARTICLE 7 - CANCELLATION AND MODIFICATION

7.1 Cancellation by the Client:

  • More than 72h before the Assignment: No charge.
  • Between 72h and 24h before the Assignment: Billing of 50% of the Assignment amount.
  • Less than 24h before the Assignment: Billing of 100% of the Assignment amount.

7.2 Cancellation by the Provider: MYSHIFTERS undertakes to actively seek a replacement Provider. If impossible, the Assignment is cancelled at no cost to the Client.
